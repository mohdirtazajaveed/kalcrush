About Block Glow

The block puzzle that plays fair.

Drag the pieces onto the 8×8 board and complete rows or columns to clear them. Every block is a sparkling jewel, and clearing a line makes them burst into light.

We fixed what players dislike in other block games: every deal is checked so the pieces can fit, game over only happens when no piece really fits, you get free Undo, and there are no ads while you play.

Features

What you'll love

Jewel blocks

Every block is a polished jewel. Clear a full row or column and watch the gems burst in a flash of light.

Fair pieces, always

Before every deal the game checks the pieces can be placed, so you never get an impossible set.

Free Undo

Made a mistake? Undo up to 3 moves every game, with no ads to watch.

500 levels, 6 scenes

Adventure through Spring Grove, Deep Jungle, Autumn Woods, Winter Frost, Cherry Garden and Firefly Night.

Daily rewards

Daily missions, a 7-day reward streak and a free daily spin with coins, hammers and shuffles.

Few ads

No banners and no ads during a game. One cheap Remove Ads purchase turns them off for good.
