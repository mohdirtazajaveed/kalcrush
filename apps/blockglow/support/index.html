Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Clearing several lines at once, or in a row, gives combo points. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.

Can I get an impossible set of pieces?

No. Before each deal the game checks that the pieces can be placed. You can still run out of space by placing badly, but never because of an unfair deal. Pieces that don't fit right now are dimmed in the tray.

How does Undo work?

Tap Undo to take back your last move. You get 3 free undos every game, with no ads to watch.

What are Classic and Adventure?

Classic is endless: play for your best score. Adventure has 500 levels with goals, such as collecting gems, and a new scene every few levels.

How do I earn coins?

Finish levels, claim the daily reward, complete daily missions and spin the free daily wheel. You can also watch an optional short video for free coins.

When do ads show?

Never during a game, and never in your first games. At most one short ad can show between games, and not more than once every few minutes. Rewarded videos are always your choice. The Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads between games.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads.

Close and reopen the game while connected to the internet so Google Play can confirm the purchase. If ads still show, email us with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.).

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Block Glow saves your progress on your phone only, with no account, so it does not move to a new phone and it is removed if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Open Settings (the slider icon at the top right) and switch each one off.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email us with your phone model, the level number and, if you can, a screenshot. We read every message.
