WordGlow privacy policy.

What information WordGlow uses, why, and the choices you have. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: WordGlow has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress stays on your device. Optional reward videos are provided by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play.

Who we are

WordGlow (“the app”) is a 3D word puzzle game published on Google Play by Kal Crush (“we”, “us”), package name com.kalcrush.wordglow. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account or any personal information, and we do not run our own servers or analytics. We never receive information about you from the app.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following on your device only, using Android's local app storage:

  • levels completed, stars and gems collected;
  • coins, hints, bonus words, daily challenge and daily spin status;
  • a history of rewards and purchases you received in the game;
  • settings such as sound, music and vibration.

This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it permanently.

Advertising

You can choose to watch a short video in the shop to get free coins. These videos are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location (country or city) derived from it;
  • device information, such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings. Videos only play when you tap to watch one.

In-app purchases

Coin and hint packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed by Google; we never see or store your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can give you your items.

Permissions

  • Internet: to load optional reward videos and to make purchases through Google Play.
  • Vibration: for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for reward videos, as described above.

Sharing your results

If you tap Share after a level, the game creates a picture of your result and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Children

WordGlow is a word game suitable for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing stored by us to access or delete. You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app, and control advertising data in your device settings. For any privacy question or request, use the Email us button below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
