About WordGlow

Crosswords that glow in 3D.

Swipe the letters on the wheel to spell words, and watch them land on crosswords that wrap around beautiful 3D gems. Every level you finish polishes a new gem for your vault.

Travel through 20 magical worlds with 500 hand-tuned levels, find bonus words, take on the daily challenge and spin the wheel for free rewards. It is calm, colourful and perfect for a quick break.
