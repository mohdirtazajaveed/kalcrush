WordGlow Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

In short: WordGlow does not ask for or store any personal information on our servers. Your game progress stays on your own device. Optional reward videos are provided by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play.

Who we are

WordGlow ("the app") is a 3D word puzzle game published on Google Play by Kal Crush (package name com.kalcrush.wordglow).

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account, name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and we do not run our own servers or analytics.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following only on your device, using Android's local app storage:

  • levels completed, stars and gems collected
  • coins, hints and daily challenge streak
  • settings such as sound, music and vibration

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it permanently.

Permissions

  • Vibration – for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Internet – used to load optional reward videos and to make purchases through Google Play.
  • Advertising ID – used by Google AdMob for reward videos (see below).

Ads and purchases

Reward videos. You can choose to watch a short video for free coins. These videos are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address and app usage data to show and measure ads. This is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings. Videos only play when you tap to watch one.

In-app purchases. Coin and hint packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see your card or payment details.

Children

WordGlow is a word game suitable for everyone. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on WordGlow's Google Play store page.
