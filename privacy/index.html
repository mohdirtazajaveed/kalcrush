Website privacy policy.

How the Kal Crush website handles information.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy covers the Kal Crush website. Each of our games has its own privacy policy, linked from its game page; for WordGlow, see the WordGlow privacy policy.

What we collect

The website does not use cookies, analytics, trackers or forms, and we do not collect personal information through it.

Fonts and hosting

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. To deliver them, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.

Emails

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and help you, and we do not share them or use them for marketing. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Links

Links to Google Play, YouTube and other sites are governed by those sites' own policies.
